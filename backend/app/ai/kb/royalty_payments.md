Royalty & Payments
- 80/20 split: 80% of net profit to the author, 20% to BookLeaf.
- Net profit = MRP minus printing cost, platform commission (Amazon/Flipkart), and shipping.
- Royalties are calculated quarterly and paid within 45 days of quarter end.
- Minimum payout is ₹1,000. Amounts below that roll to the next quarter.
- Payouts go by bank transfer to the account on the author dashboard.
- Authors can see a per-platform royalty breakdown in the dashboard.

Sample
Q: I published 4 months ago and still have not received any royalty.
A: Acknowledge frustration. Explain the quarterly cycle and 45-day window. Check if bank details are linked. Give the next payout date if known. If genuinely overdue, escalate with a 48-hour resolution timeline.

Q: I sold 200 copies but only received ₹3,000.
A: Explain net profit (MRP minus printing, commission, shipping). Offer a line-by-line breakdown. Do not be defensive.
