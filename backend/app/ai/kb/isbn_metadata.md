ISBN & Metadata Issues
- Every BookLeaf book receives a unique ISBN assigned by BookLeaf.
- ISBNs are registered under BookLeaf's imprint. An author who wants their own imprint must obtain that ISBN independently.
- Duplicate ISBN or a wrong book linked to an ISBN is high priority and is escalated to production immediately.

Sample
Q: Amazon shows a different ISBN than the physical copy.
A: Treat as high priority. Acknowledge it is a serious data issue. Escalate to production immediately. Promise a 48-hour resolution timeline.
