Distribution & Availability
- Books list on Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK, and the BookLeaf Store.
- New listings typically go live 7–10 business days after publication is complete.
- "Currently Unavailable" is usually a stock sync issue. The team can trigger a re-sync in 24–48 hours.

Sample
Q: My published book shows Currently Unavailable on Amazon.
A: Explain stock sync. Confirm you are triggering a re-sync. Set 24–48 hours for it to go live again.
