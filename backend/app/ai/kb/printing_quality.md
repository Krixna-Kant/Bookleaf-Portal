Printing & Quality
- Most orders print in-house in Delhi. Overflow or special formats go to Repro India or Epitome Books.
- Standard print turnaround is 5–7 business days from order confirmation.
- Quality issues (misprints, binding defects, color inconsistency) get a free reprint after verification. Ask the author for photos of the defective copy.
